Resources and Support

Online resources

  • Homepage: https://www.gnuhealth.org

  • Mastodon: For regular news updates, follow the official GNU Health account on Mastodon at: https://mastodon.social/@gnuhealth

  • Mailing lists at GNU: There are several GNU Health mailing lists at GNU Savannah. The most relevant are “health” for general discussions and “health-dev” for developers. Please note that you need to subscribe. Emails of non-members are automatically discarded. To subscribe or view the archives of these mailing lists, please visit https://savannah.gnu.org/mail/?group=health

  • Libera Chat IRC: The official GNU Health for asking questions interactively related to the GNU Health ecosystem. Join the #gnu-health channel https://libera.chat/

  • Matrix: There are rooms for some of the GNUHealth components https://matrix.to/#/#gnuhealth-space:matrix.org

Training and support for elegible projects

GNU Solidario can provide training and implementation support to elegible institutions.

In order to be elegible, the following requirements must be met:

  • An introduction of the current needs

  • The project will use free/Libre software

  • There will be a local designated person that will be in charge of the project and the know-how transfer to the rest of the community.This person must be committed to be from the beginning of the project until two years after its completion.

Please send your project support request to: info@gnuhealth.org
