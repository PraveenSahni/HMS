1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how IRISSTAR Technologies (“IST,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), operating the IST Health hospital and clinic management platform (the “Platform”), collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information.

The Platform is provided to healthcare facilities and authorized users in Qatar and may be accessed by staff, administrators, and—where enabled—patients. The application display name is IST Technologies LLC / IST Health.

3. Scope

This policy applies to personal information processed through the IST Health Platform, including web interfaces, GNU Health backend systems, and demo/UAT or production environments operated by or on behalf of IST.

4. Information We Collect

Patient and clinical data

Identity and demographics, medical history, appointments, laboratory orders and results, pharmacy records, billing information, and uploaded documents. Demo/UAT environments may use synthetic or anonymized test data.

Staff and administrator data

Names, credentials, roles, account information, authentication logs, and contact details.

Technical data

IP address, device and browser information, session logs, audit trails, and configuration preferences.

6. Qatar and GCC Context

We aim to align with recognized data protection principles: lawfulness, purpose limitation, data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity, confidentiality, and accountability.

Qatar’s Personal Data Privacy Protection Law (PDPL) and healthcare regulations may apply. This draft has not been verified for regulatory compliance. Healthcare facilities remain responsible for their own obligations, including patient notices and consent.

7. Data Storage and Security

Platform data is hosted on Google Cloud Platform (GCP), with primary compute resources in the me-central1 region (Middle East).

Security measures may include TLS encryption, role-based access controls, authentication requirements, audit logging, backups, and network monitoring. No system is completely secure; we apply measures appropriate to health information sensitivity.

8. Data Retention

Retention depends on healthcare facility requirements, applicable law, and environment type (production vs. demo/UAT). Demo data may be refreshed or deleted periodically. Production data is retained per facility policy and contractual terms.

9. Sharing and Third Parties

We may share information with GCP (hosting), GNU Health open-source components, the healthcare facility, authorized subprocessors, and legal authorities when required. We do not sell personal information.

10. GNU Health

The Platform is built on GNU Health, licensed under the GNU General Public License (GPL). IST branding, customizations, and hosting remain subject to separate terms.

11. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing. Patients should direct clinical record requests to their healthcare provider. Staff may contact info@isttechnologies.qa for account-related inquiries.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date reflects material changes. Continued use after updates constitutes acknowledgment where permitted by law.
